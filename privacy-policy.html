Who we are

aibrainstartup.com is operated by AI BRAIN STARTUPS, a micro enterprise registered with the Government of India (Udyam UDYAM-RJ-05-0067280), Rajasthan, India. In this policy “we” means AI BRAIN STARTUPS.

What we collect

We only collect what you send us:

  • Waitlist: your email address and the role you select.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, inquiry type and message.
  • With each submission: the time, your approximate country (derived by our hosting provider from your IP address), your browser’s user-agent string and the page you came from.
  • Abuse protection: a one-way hash of your IP address, kept for rate limiting and deleted automatically within about 24 hours.

We do not store your raw IP address with your submission.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical request logs for security and reliability.

Why we use it

To give you early access and send updates about AI Brain, to reply to your messages, and to protect the site from spam and abuse. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Where it is stored

The site and its forms are hosted by Vercel Inc. Submissions are stored in a private Vercel Blob store located in the United States (Washington, D.C. region), accessible only to us. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by the provider.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist entries: until you ask us to delete them, or until early access ends.
  • Contact messages: up to 24 months, unless an ongoing conversation needs longer.
  • IP hashes used for rate limiting: about 24 hours.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent at any time, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Email founder@aibrainstartup.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
