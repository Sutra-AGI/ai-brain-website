When to choose what

Pragmatic engineering guidance.

Use Today

Shipping immediately

If your agent stack needs an installable pip/npm package in production this week for lightweight chat personalization or bi-temporal enterprise timeline tracking, evaluate established open-source offerings like Mem0, Zep/Graphiti, Letta, or Cognee.

Flat Vector Limit

When cosine RAG breaks

If your multi-agent system loses architectural constraints across 100+ turns, fails multi-hop code provenance lookups, or suffers runaway context replay bills, flat chunk retrieval cannot express N-ary relationships without graph structure.
