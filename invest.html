Due Diligence FAQ

Frequently asked investor questions.

Everything you need to know about our legal structure, wire settlement, and revenue payouts.

What legal instrument governs this investment?

We use standard iSAFE (India Simple Agreement for Future Equity) contracts, modeled on the Silicon Valley Y-Combinator SAFE. It grants you verified equity ownership rights with contractual CCPS conversion upon incorporation.

How does the 3.0x–3.5x revenue-share dividend work?

In addition to your permanent equity stake, you receive a contractual quarterly dividend from gross top-line software revenue (5% to 15% depending on tier) until you have received exactly 3.0x to 3.5x of your principal capital back.

What happens upon a Series A round or strategic acquisition?

Under Article 2.2 of our iSAFE agreement, an institutional Series A or acquisition triggers a senior liquidity event. Investors receive either full accelerated cash payoff of their remaining return cap or convert their stake into preferred stock at a 25% discount to the Series A valuation, whichever yields higher value.

Can international investors from USA, EU, or Brazil participate?

Yes. International inward remittances in USD, EUR, or GBP are processed through standard SWIFT bank wires. Our bank issues a Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) confirming your cross-border investment under RBI compliance.

Can multiple angel investors syndicate together into one slot?

Yes. Angel syndicates or collectives can combine capital to participate in Tier 3 ($50,000 USD) or Tier 4 ($100,000 USD). A single lead entity or nominee structure is executed with pro-rata distribution rights defined in Annexure A.
