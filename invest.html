Due Diligence FAQ

Frequently asked investor questions.

Everything you need to know about our legal structure, wire settlement, and revenue payouts.

What legal instrument governs this investment?

We use standard iSAFE (India Simple Agreement for Future Equity) contracts, modeled on the Silicon Valley Y-Combinator SAFE. It grants you verified equity ownership rights without requiring messy immediate cap-table restructuring.

How does the 3.0x–3.5x revenue-share dividend work?

In addition to your permanent equity stake, you receive a contractual monthly dividend from gross top-line software revenue (5% to 15% depending on tier) until you have received exactly 3.0x to 3.5x of your principal capital back.

Can international investors from USA, EU, or Brazil participate?

Yes. International inward remittances in USD, EUR, or GBP are processed through standard SWIFT bank wires. Our bank issues a Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) confirming your cross-border investment under RBI compliance.

When does operational execution begin?

Immediately upon wire clearance. The 90-day deployment schedule is triggered on Day 1: senior engineering hiring begins, dedicated GPU instances are spun up, and intellectual property filings are lodged.

Investment is received as a Pre-Incorporation Convertible Advance. Upon closing of the round, the Founder contractually covenants to incorporate AI Brain Startups Private Limited within 30 days and allot corresponding Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS) or Equity Shares to the Investor.

Clause 2: Contractual Revenue-Share Hurdle (3.0x–3.5x)

Prior to and independent of equity liquidity events, the Company shall disburse 5% to 15% (per allocated tier) of top-line Enterprise Software Licensing & Memory Node API receipts on a quarterly basis until 300% to 350% of the principal capital has been returned in cash.

For international allocations (USA, EU, Brazil), funds are processed via SWIFT under RBI FEMA Purpose Code P0802 (Computer & Information Services / Software R&D). Official Foreign Inward Remittance Certificates (FIRC) will be delivered within 48 hours of credit.
