Due Diligence FAQ

Frequently asked investor questions.

Everything you need to know about our legal structure, wire settlement, and revenue payouts.

What legal instrument governs this investment?

We use standard iSAFE (India Simple Agreement for Future Equity) contracts, modeled on the Silicon Valley Y-Combinator SAFE. It grants you verified equity ownership rights with contractual CCPS conversion upon incorporation.

How does the 2.5x–3.0x revenue-share dividend work?

In addition to your permanent equity stake, you receive a contractual quarterly dividend from gross top-line software revenue (5% to 12% depending on tier) until you have received exactly 2.5x to 3.0x of your principal capital back.

What happens if the company fails or has to shut down?

Under our iSAFE contract (Article 2.3), in any dissolution event, all remaining unspent treasury funds are returned pro-rata to investors first (Senior Liquidation Preference). Legitimate engineering R&D is protected by limited liability law, backed by a 100% bank audit trail.

How do I know my capital won't be misused?

100% of proceeds reside in our official State Bank of India account (A/c 40318275832). Under iSAFE Article 1.2, promoter personal loans, dividends, or luxury expenses are contractually barred. Backers receive monthly financial flash reports.

Can international investors from USA, EU, or Brazil participate?

Yes. International inward remittances in USD, EUR, or GBP are processed through standard SWIFT bank wires. Our bank issues a Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) confirming your cross-border investment under RBI compliance.

Can I speak directly with the Founder before transferring?

Yes. We encourage direct due diligence. You can reach Bhoja Ram directly on WhatsApp (+91 94611 27967) or email founder@aibrainstartup.com to schedule a 1-on-1 technical review.

Why invest in AI Brain instead of flat vector databases like Pinecone?

Vector databases only store flat embeddings and re-ingest entire prompts on every turn, burning thousands in token costs. AI Brain's neuromorphic hypergraph delivers sub-15ms associative recall with a 91% reduction in token burn.

When does operational execution begin?

Immediately upon wire clearance. The 90-day deployment schedule is triggered on Day 1: senior engineering hiring begins, dedicated GPU instances are spun up, and intellectual property filings are lodged.
