Graph Retrieval

Why Cosine Similarity Fails Multi-Hop Agent Reasoning

Single-step approximate nearest neighbor search retrieves lexically or topically adjacent fragments — not causal reasoning chains across multi-session timelines.

Published 2026-11-266 min readBy Bhoja Ram Choudhary

1. The Transitivity Blind Spot in Dense Vectors

Consider a real engineering workflow spanning three sessions over two weeks:

  • Session 1 (Day 1): An architect specifies that tenant payment settlement logs must stay inside asia-south1 for regulatory residency.
  • Session 14 (Day 9): A service module settlement_worker.py is introduced to process async webhooks.
  • Session 42 (Day 14): A coding agent is asked: “Where should we deploy the new async webhook worker?”

Notice the geometric failure: the query embedding mentions “async webhook worker deployment”. Session 1’s policy node mentions “payment settlement logs residency”. Their pairwise cosine similarity is frequently below the top-k cutoff because the bridging entity (settlement_worker.py) lives in Session 14.

2. Why Larger Top-K and Rerankers Only Mask the Bug

Standard vector RAG pipelines try to patch missing bridges by fetching top-k = 40 or 80 chunks and feeding them to a cross-encoder reranker. That approach runs into two walls:

  1. Quadratic dilution ("lost in the middle"): Flooding the prompt with 25,000+ semi-relevant tokens degrades attention over rare constraint rules.
  2. Broken cross-chunk provenance: Even when Session 1 and Session 14 appear separately, nothing structurally proves that Session 1 constrains Session 14’s deployment target unless a relational edge connects them.

3. Associative N-ary Hypergraphs as the Retrieval Substrate

In AI Brain’s planned associative hypergraph model, an atomic memory is not an isolated chunk — it participates in typed hyperedges binding actors, intents, files, policies, and temporal validity together.

Recall(Q) = ExpandEdges(ANN_Seeds(Q), depth=4, decay_guard=True) -> BoundedSubgraph(k)

When recall() seeds on settlement_worker.py via vector similarity, traversing typed edges over 2 to 4 hops surfaces the upstream asia-south1 residency policy automatically — without scanning unrelated turns.
