ARTICLE I: ALLOCATION OF PROCEEDS & CAPITAL DISCIPLINE

1.1 Strict Capital Deployment Mandate: One hundred percent (100%) of all funds invested pursuant to this Agreement shall be deployed exclusively in furtherance of core technology development and commercial enterprise onboarding under the Company's 90-Day Execution Roadmap:

  • 50% Core Engineering: Competitive compensation and equity retention for elite distributed systems, C++, and neuromorphic graph architects.
  • 30% High-Throughput Compute & Hardware: Dedicated GPU inference clusters, high-speed NVMe flash memory arrays, and global edge CDN instances.
  • 10% Patents & Intellectual Property: International Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) priority filings and comprehensive trademark protection.
  • 10% Developer Ecosystem: SDK documentation, open benchmark verifications, and enterprise developer onboarding hackathons.

1.2 Prohibition of Promoter Extraction: The Founder explicitly covenants that no portion of pre-seed round proceeds shall be utilized for promoter loans, personal living expenses, luxury corporate amenities, or non-operational vanity expenses.

ARTICLE II: PRIVATE LIMITED INCORPORATION & EQUITY CONVERSION COVENANT

2.1 Mandatory Corporate Restructuring: Within thirty (30) days following the formal closure of this Pre-Seed Round, the Founder covenants to incorporate AI Brain Startups Private Limited (or such approved statutory corporate designation) under the Companies Act, 2013, Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India.

2.2 Conversion Mechanics (CCPS / Equity Shares): Upon the closing of the Company's next Qualified Institutional Equity Financing Round (raising gross proceeds exceeding $1,000,000 USD), this iSAFE instrument shall automatically convert into Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS) or Series Seed Equity Shares at a price per share equal to the lesser of:

  • (a) The price calculated by dividing the Post-Money Valuation Cap ($4,000,000 USD) by the fully diluted share capital of the Company immediately prior to the financing round; or
  • (b) The per-share price paid by new institutional investors in such Qualified Financing discounted by exactly twenty-five percent (25.0%).

2.3 Senior Liquidity & Dissolution Preference: In the event of an acquisition, merger, corporate sale of substantially all assets, or dissolution prior to equity conversion, the Investor shall possess senior liquidation preference over all ordinary equity holders to receive the greater of: (i) one hundred percent (100%) of the Original Principal Amount plus any accrued unpaid revenue-share distributions, or (ii) the fair market valuation of the underlying shares had this instrument converted at the Valuation Cap.

ARTICLE III: CONTRACTUAL ACCELERATED REVENUE-SHARE DISTRIBUTIONS

3.1 Senior Cash Distribution Stream: Distinct from and in addition to permanent equity conversion rights, the Company undertakes an irrevocable legal covenant to disburse five percent (5.0%) to fifteen percent (15.0%) (depending upon the subscribed tier) of its top-line gross software licensing, graph memory API, and enterprise support billings directly to the Investor's designated settlement bank account.

3.2 Hurdle Multiplier & Timing: Revenue disbursements shall be computed within ten (10) calendar days of each calendar quarter end and wired within fifteen (15) days thereof, continuing until cumulative payouts equal exactly the contracted Return Multiplier (3.0x for Tiers 1, 2 and 3; 3.5x for Tier 4). Upon reaching the Return Cap, cash revenue distributions shall terminate, while the Investor's permanent equity ownership stake and voting rights remain intact in perpetuity.

ARTICLE IV: FOREIGN REMITTANCE, RBI & FEMA COMPLIANCE

4.1 Authorized International Banking Channel: For cross-border capital remittances (originating from the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, Brazil, Singapore, or other international jurisdictions), all funds shall be transmitted exclusively via bank-to-bank SWIFT wire through State Bank of India (Forex Hub: SBININBB154) under Reserve Bank of India FEMA Purpose Code P0802 (Software Technology Services & Computer Systems R&D).

4.2 FIRC Certification: The handling bank shall formally generate and the Company shall deliver an official Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) confirming sovereign inward clearance under Indian foreign exchange law within forty-eight (48) banking hours of receipt.

ARTICLE V: INFORMATION RIGHTS, FINANCIAL AUDIT & GOVERNANCE

5.1 Investor Reporting Package: The Company shall deliver: (a) Monthly financial flash reports detailing monthly burn rate, cash runway, and engineering roadmap progress; (b) Quarterly unaudited financial statements within 30 days of quarter end; and (c) Annual audited statutory financial statements certified by an independent Chartered Accountant.

5.2 Board Observer Status: Investors subscribing at Tier 3 ($50,000 USD) and above shall have the contractual right to appoint one Advisory Board Observer to attend quarterly executive reviews and technical briefings with the Founder.

ARTICLE VI: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & JURISDICTION

6.1 Exclusive IP Assignment: The Founder represents and warrants that 100% of all software repositories, associative graph codebases, mathematical weights, documentation, and international patent applications are the sole, unencumbered property of AI Brain Startups and will be seamlessly transferred to the Private Limited entity.

6.2 Governing Law & Binding Arbitration: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of India. Any controversy or claim arising hereunder shall be finally settled by binding arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, conducted in the English language, seated in Jaipur / New Delhi, India.
