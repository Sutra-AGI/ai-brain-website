INVESTOR PRIVACY, KYC & BANKING CONFIDENTIALITY POLICY

Comprehensive Institutional Guidelines on the Secure Collection, Cryptographic Custody, and Statutory Handling of Investor Identity, Wire Transmissions, and Cap-Table Ledger Data

This Investor Privacy, KYC & Banking Confidentiality Policy (the "Policy") sets forth the binding legal commitments and information security practices maintained by AI BRAIN STARTUPS (together with its forthcoming incorporated entity AI Brain Startups Private Limited, the "Company") with respect to accredited angel allocators, family offices, sovereign syndicates, and institutional investment entities (each an "Investor").

We adhere strictly to the highest standards of financial privacy under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (including the Information Technology Reasonable Security Practices Rules), the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP), and international financial data protection norms (including GDPR compliance for European subscribers).

ARTICLE I: SCOPE & PARTICIPANT CLASSIFICATION

1.1 Exclusivity: This Policy applies strictly and exclusively to persons and entities accessing the AI Brain Investor Portal (aibrainstartup.com/invest) and subscribing to the Pre-Seed Strategic Round via the India Simple Agreement for Future Equity (iSAFE) instrument.
1.2 Non-Consumer Segregation: General website visitor tracking and product analytics (governed separately under the public General Privacy Policy) are completely air-gapped from the financial investor database. Investor financial records are held in an isolated, encrypted partition with restricted executive access.

ARTICLE II: CATEGORIES OF INVESTOR DATA COLLECTED

2.1 Know Your Customer (KYC) Credentials: Full legal name, date of birth, residential or corporate address, government-issued identification (Permanent Account Number [PAN] / Passport / Driver's License / Corporate Tax ID), and legal authorized signatory documentation.
2.2 Banking & Remittance Records: Bank name, originating account holder title, SWIFT / BIC codes, domestic UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) codes, Fedwire/IMAD numbers, and certified bank wire remittance advices.
2.3 Allocation & Cap-Table Records: Dollar/Rupee principal committed, assigned tranche (Tier 1 through Tier 4), issued iSAFE equity ownership percentage (0.375% to 2.50%), and quarterly top-line gross revenue-share dividend distribution ledger numbers.

ARTICLE III: STRICT PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

3.1 Statutory Regulatory Compliance: Ensuring full adherence to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) guidelines for inward foreign direct remittances under Purpose Code P0802 (Software & Computer Services Technology R&D).
3.2 Issuance of Sovereign Certificates: Facilitating the automatic generation of official Foreign Inward Remittance Certificates (FIRC) and Foreign Inward Remittance Advices (FIRA) through our handling corporate banker, the State Bank of India.
3.3 Corporate Incorporation & Share Allocation: Executing the 30-day covenant under the Companies Act, 2013 to formally register Investor holdings as Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS) with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).
3.4 Dividend Payout Transmission: Facilitating quarterly bank-to-bank gross revenue distributions (10% gross top-line pool) until the contractual 3.0x to 3.5x hurdle cap is reached.

ARTICLE IV: ABSOLUTE CONFIDENTIALITY & ZERO DISCLOSURE COVENANT

4.1 Zero Commercial Exploitation: The Company contractually warrants that Investor data, contact credentials, financial net worth disclosures, or wire documentation shall NEVER be sold, rented, leased, licensed, or shared with third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or brokers.
4.2 Confidential Cap-Table Protection: Except as required by statutory mandate to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), or a formal court order having competent jurisdiction, individual investor allocation sizes and identities remain strictly confidential between the Investor and Founder Bhoja Ram Choudhary.
4.3 Anonymized Reporting: Backers receiving monthly financial flash reports and operational updates are presented with aggregate pool metrics without disclosing individual identities or private wire references of co-investors, unless explicit written consent is granted.

ARTICLE V: DATA RETENTION, SECURITY & STATUTORY AUDIT

5.1 AES-256 Storage Encryption: All investor identification and wire documentation are stored using AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
5.2 Statutory Retention Period: Under the Companies Act, 2013 and Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), financial records, banking ledgers, and iSAFE subscription agreements must be preserved for a statutory period of 8 (eight) financial years from the date of settlement.
5.3 Investor Audit Rights: Every accredited investor maintaining active iSAFE equity rights retains the right to inspect their personal entry in the official register of members and obtain an updated certificate of allocation upon written request to founder@aibrainstartup.com.
